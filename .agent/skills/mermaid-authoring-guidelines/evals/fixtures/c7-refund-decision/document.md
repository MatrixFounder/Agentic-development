## 6. Refund decision

A customer asks for a refund in the support portal of Mossgiel Outfitters.
This section decides every refund request. The checks R6.1, R6.2 and R6.3 run in this order.

### 6.1 Rules

- **R6.1** A request made more than 30 days after delivery is declined.
  An item that the customer reports as defective goes to a support agent, regardless of its age.
- **R6.2** A digital good is refundable only if it has not been downloaded.
  R6.2 applies to digital goods only.
- **R6.3** A refund of $50 or less is approved automatically.
- **R6.4** A refund above $50 goes to a support agent, who approves or declines it.
- **R6.5** A refund above $500 that a support agent approves also needs a team lead.
  The team lead approves or declines it.
- **R6.6** An approved refund goes back to the original payment method.

The support portal records every decision in the history of the request.

### 6.2 Limits from other sections

| Subject | Limit | Section |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Exchange of an item | within 60 days of delivery | §5 |
| Store credit | up to $200, issued instantly | §7 |
| Answer of a support agent | within 2 business days | §7 |
| Payout to the customer | within 5 business days | §7 |

These limits do not change the refund decision of §6.1.
